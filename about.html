About Us • हमारे बारे में

Built Inside Real School Administration—Not a Software Bubble

PoshanKosh® (पोषणकोष) was not dreamt up in a distant tech office by people who have never held a school cashbook. It was shaped by years of watching Headmasters, Nodal Teachers, and school clerks juggle two demanding responsibilities at once: teaching children all day, and spending every evening reconciling PM-POSHAN registers by hand.

Across India’s 28 States and 8 Union Territories, schools follow strict accounting norms—100g and 150g rice calculations, ₹6.78 and ₹10.17 cooking cost slabs, caste-wise attendance sheets, bank passbooks, and monthly vouchers—yet teachers are still expected to manage all of it with pen, paper, and pocket calculators. PoshanKosh® exists to end that burden cleanly.

1. One Offline Workspace for Every Statutory Register

Most generic spreadsheet apps fall short the moment you ask them to handle a school’s real-world Mid-Day Meal rules. PoshanKosh® was engineered from the ground up around the exact statutory workflow teachers follow every month:

12+ Statutory Registers & Quick Restock

Covers Daily Expenditure, Cashbook, Rice, Ragi, Egg/Fruit, IFA Iron, Chakhna (Tasting), Weekly, Monthly MPR, Quarterly UC, Half-Yearly, and Annual Audit registers—plus 3-dot Quick Restock with automatic KG–5 and 6–8 totals.

CA-Verified Cashbook & Bank Match

Maintains a chronological DD-MM-YYYY cashbook with Bank-to-Cash Cheque Withdrawals, Split-Expense Scheme Ledgers, Bank Interest and Charge entries, 1-to-1 Statement auto-fill, and the Royal Blue Verified Seal.

1-Click Monthly Vouchers & MPR PDF

Generates all 4 Monthly Vouchers (Ration, Egg/Fruit, Vegetables, and Fuel — Vr 1 to Vr 4) along with print-ready 2-page Hindi/English MPR PDFs without re-entering a single figure.

Built for Zero-Signal Classrooms

Works 100% offline on standard Android phones with 7-day low-cash alerts, caste and gender attendance summaries, and instant encrypted backup whenever you choose to sync.

2. Four Commitments We Never Compromise On

Trust is everything when a school relies on an app for its daily accounts. Rather than hiding behind vague marketing lines, we hold ourselves to four plain, verifiable standards:

  • Complete Financial Privacy: We never store your cashbook, bank statements, scheme grants, or stock balances on our servers. All financial records stay encrypted locally on your device and in your school’s own Google Drive (drive.appdata).
  • Strict Independence: PoshanKosh® is an individual initiative created solely to assist teachers. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government portal or ministry, and makes no claim to be a government app.
  • Zero Ads, Zero Data Selling: Teachers opening a register at school should never have to dodge pop-up advertisements or worry about who is reading their records.
  • Transparent, Honest Pricing: The modest ₹119 contribution exists strictly to cover cloud servers, deployment, domain maintenance, and ongoing updates—keeping the platform clean, fast, and sustainable.
Our Goal Is Simple

When a teacher wraps up the school day, their registers should already be balanced, their vouchers ready, and their evening free. Everything inside PoshanKosh® is built to make that ordinary peace of mind a daily reality.
