About PoshanKosh (हमारे बारे में)

PoshanKosh® (पोषणकोष) is an intelligent digital management and accounting platform created for headmasters and teachers across government and aided schools in India.

Under the national PM-POSHAN (Mid-Day Meal) scheme, school educators spend hours manually calculating rice grams, cooking allowances, voucher bills, and bank passbooks. PoshanKosh cuts administrative workload by 90% through automated, transparent mathematics.

Our Core Principles:
✓ Teacher-First Simplicity: Complex registers converted into single-tap actions.
✓ Zero Financial Errors: 100% matched bank statement and cashbook equations.
✓ Local Sovereignty: Data stays on school devices and private school Google Drive.
