1. Definitions & Interpretation

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  • "Application" / "PoshanKosh®" refers to the Android mobile application (in.poshankosh.teacher), companion web pages (poshankosh.com), and associated licensing APIs developed and maintained as an individual software utility.
  • "User" / "Principal" means the adult Headmaster, Teacher-in-Charge, or authorized school operator registering and operating a school profile on PoshanKosh®.
  • "Financial & Operational Records" means all Cashbook entries, Bank Statements, Opening/Closing balances, Rice/Egg/Ragi/IFA stock registers, Withdrawal records, Scheme split-expense ledgers, Monthly Vouchers, and MPR PDFs created by the User.
  • "School Metadata" means the minimal administrative details required to identify a school UDISE tenant and issue a valid software license.

2. Scope, Applicability & Legal Basis

This Privacy Policy is published in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India. By downloading, installing, or registering on PoshanKosh®, you provide explicit, informed consent to the processing of minimal registration metadata strictly for the purposes stated herein.

3. Zero Financial Transaction Storage Guarantee

PoshanKosh® operates on a strict Zero-Knowledge Financial Architecture. We do not upload, inspect, analyze, aggregate, or store any of your school's financial transactions on our cloud servers. Specifically, our servers never receive:

  • Bank account numbers, passbook balances, or uploaded Bank Statement PDFs/CSVs/images;
  • Cash-in-hand balances, Bank Interest or Bank Charge entries, or Withdrawal Cheque numbers;
  • Scheme grant amounts, split-expense installments, or MPR / Cashbook PDF exports;
  • Daily rice consumption, cooking cost calculations, or vendor payment vouchers.

4. Categories of Minimal Metadata We Collect

We collect only the strictly necessary registration and licensing metadata:

  • School Identity Metadata: Official School Name, 11-digit UDISE code, State/Union Territory (across all 28 States & 8 UTs), District, Block, Panchayat/CRC, 6-digit Pincode, and configured class range (e.g., KG–5, 6–8).
  • Authorized Operator Details: Principal / Headmaster Name, 10-digit contact mobile number, verified Google email address, and Google Account Subject Identifier (google_sub).
  • Subscription & Licensing Telemetry: Trial start/expiry timestamps, payment order references, plan tier, and server-side administrative audit events (such as school approval or authorized Principal account transfer).
  • App Integrity Signals: Installed package version code and APK signing certificate SHA-256 hash, used solely to prevent unauthorized modified APKs and version-downgrade attacks.

5. Offline-First Local Encrypted Storage (sqlite3mc)

All school operational and financial records reside exclusively inside a local, AES-encrypted SQLite database (sqlite3mc) on your Android device. Database encryption keys, session tokens, and offline license grants are stored inside Android's hardware-backed Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences so that unauthorized third-party apps cannot read your school registers.

6. Google Sign-In & Google Drive API (drive.appdata) Limited Use Disclosure

PoshanKosh® integrates Google OAuth 2.0 Sign-In (openid, email) and the restricted Google Drive App Data scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata):

  • Purpose of drive.appdata: Enables the Principal to back up and restore encrypted school database snapshots inside a hidden, app-specific folder within the school's own Google Drive.
  • Zero Access to Personal Drive Files: PoshanKosh® cannot view, list, read, edit, or delete any personal documents, photos, spreadsheets, or folders stored in your Google Drive.
  • Client-Side Encryption: Backups are encrypted on your device with your recovery passphrase before leaving your phone. Neither PoshanKosh® nor Google can read the contents of your encrypted backup file.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: PoshanKosh®'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never use Google user data for targeted advertising, data brokering, or training generalized AI/ML models.

7. On-Device Document OCR & PDF Processing

When you import Bank Statements (PDF, CSV, Excel, or camera photographs) for 1-to-1 Cashbook verification, or upload Annual Holiday Lists, all parsing and Optical Character Recognition (using Google MLKit on-device Text Recognition) occurs 100% locally on your device. Temporary rendered page images are automatically deleted immediately after extraction.

8. Device Permissions & Local Push Notifications

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used strictly on-device to alert the Principal when Cash in Hand falls to 7 days or less of average daily expenditure, or for scheduled scheme reminders.
  • File / Media Picker: Accessed only when you manually select a bank statement, holiday list, or export a generated PDF/Excel report.
  • Network State / Internet: Used for initial authentication, license refresh, optional market-rate lookups, and user-initiated Google Drive backup/restore.

9. Payment Gateways & Cryptographic Offline Licensing

Subscription payments are processed exclusively through RBI-compliant payment gateways (such as PhonePe). PoshanKosh® never receives, processes, or stores your UPI PIN, credit/debit card number, CVV, or bank login credentials. Once a payment or authorized trial is verified, our server issues an Ed25519 cryptographically signed license grant cached locally on your device so you can work offline without interruption.

10. Cloud Infrastructure & Sub-Processors

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade any user data. Minimal registration and licensing metadata is stored on reputable cloud infrastructure providers (Render for API hosting and Neon Serverless PostgreSQL for database storage) protected by TLS 1.2/1.3 encryption in transit and encrypted storage at rest. Upon new school registration, an administrative notification containing only the School Name, UDISE code, and Principal email may be dispatched to our internal admin alert channel for onboarding support.

11. Student & Children's Privacy

PoshanKosh® is a B2B/institutional utility intended strictly for adult school Principals and teachers—never for use by children under 18. Attendance and caste/gender summaries (SC/ST/OBC/General) are stored solely in aggregate form on the Principal's device and inside the school's encrypted Drive backup; no individual child's personal data is transmitted to our servers.

12. Data Retention & Principal Account Transfers

School registration metadata is retained for as long as the school maintains an active or historical UDISE binding, to prevent unauthorized duplicate registrations. In the event of Principal transfer or retirement, a verified account transfer may be executed to bind the UDISE code to the incoming Principal's school Google account while preserving audit continuity.

13. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act 2023)

Under applicable Indian data protection law, you have the right to: (a) access and review your stored school registration metadata; (b) request correction of inaccurate UDISE, Principal, or location details; (c) withdraw consent and request permanent deletion of your server-side registration metadata (which will unlink your school from cloud licensing); and (d) erase all local device data immediately by uninstalling the app.

14. Security & Anti-Tamper Controls

We enforce strict technical safeguards including encrypted local storage, Ed25519 signature validation, certificate pinning, and monotonic anti-downgrade checks. Users remain responsible for maintaining a strong device lock screen and safeguarding their Google account and Drive recovery passphrase.

15. Policy Amendments

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect legal, regulatory, or functional enhancements. Material changes will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date.
